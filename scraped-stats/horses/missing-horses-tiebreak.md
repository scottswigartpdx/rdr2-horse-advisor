# Missing horses tie-break (Story Mode)

Goal: confirm which “missing coats” are real, and identify any **source naming errors**.

## White Brindle Arabian vs Warped Brindle Arabian
- **IGN (PC Exclusive Horses table)** lists: “White Brindle Arabian” near Wapiti.
  - Source: `https://www.ign.com/wikis/red-dead-redemption-2/Horses_Guide_-_Locations,_Stats,_Bonding,_Taming,_and_Breeds`
- **IGN (Arabian breed page)** only lists 3 coats (Rose Grey Bay / Black / White) and does **not** list White Brindle.
  - Source: `https://www.ign.com/wikis/red-dead-redemption-2/Arabian`
- **Fandom (Arabian breed page)** lists **Warped Brindle** (and does not list White Brindle).
  - Source: `https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Arabian`
- **rdr2.org** explicitly lists “Warped Brindle Arabian” as a rare wild horse above Wapiti.
  - Source: `https://www.rdr2.org/guides/story-mode/wild-horse-locations-guide/`
- **Shacknews** location guide names it “Warped Brindle Arabian”.
  - Source: `https://www.shacknews.com/article/114919/warped-brindle-arabian-horse-location-in-red-dead-redemption-2`
- **Gamepur** location guide names it “Warped Brindle Arabian”.
  - Source: `https://www.gamepur.com/guides/warped-brindle-arabian-red-dead-redemption-2-location`

Conclusion: treat IGN’s “White Brindle Arabian” as a **naming error** for **Warped Brindle Arabian**. Do **not** add a duplicate entry.
