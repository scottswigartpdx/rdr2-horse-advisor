# RDR2 Story Mode — Stable Horse Inventory (research notes)

Purpose: a **Story Mode–only** reference list of **horses sold at each stable** (breed/coat + price + chapter gating) for reconciling `horses.json` acquisition/price fields.

## Sources used (primary)
- **Red Dead Wiki (Fandom)** stable/vendor pages (each page includes a “Horses for Sale” table with prices and chapter notes)
- **IGN** breed pages (used mainly to identify/flag conflicts like the Bay Roan Morgan “stable” ambiguity)

All source URLs are listed per-stable below.

## Notes / conventions
- **CH3 / CH4 / CH6**: Fandom uses these to denote the earliest Story chapter the horse becomes purchasable at that stable.
- **Epilogue-only**: Fandom stable pages explicitly note if the stable’s horses are purchasable only during the epilogue.
- **SUE**: Special/Ultimate Edition exclusive (“Free” purchase at Valentine stable per Fandom stable page notes).
- **POB**: Pre-order bonus exclusive (“Free” purchase at Valentine stable per Fandom stable page notes).
- This file lists **what the stable sells** (purchase inventory), not wild/steal/encounter acquisitions.

## Known conflicts / follow-ups

### Bay Roan Morgan “purchasable at Valentine Stable?”
- **IGN** (Morgan breed page) includes “Chapter 2 – Valentine Stable” in its “Found” column for Bay Roan, but does not clearly distinguish “sold” vs “present near the stable / owned”.
  - Source: `https://www.ign.com/wikis/red-dead-redemption-2/Morgan`
- **Fandom** (Morgan breed page) explicitly says the compendium’s “Bay Roan can be bought from the stables” is **Online-only**, not Story Mode, and that “Exit Pursued by a Bruised Ego” is the only Story Mode instance of buying a Morgan at a stable (Palomino, mission-only).
  - Source: `https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Morgan`
